v7 paper inputs (public, no login). Official P&L starts on the first
complete D1 on or after freeze-date 2026-11-16. Pre-freeze days are
as-if reference only.

Policy rates (BIS WS_CBPOL, previous-month use, no look-ahead):
  python3 scripts/v6-rates.py data/v7/rates-paper.csv data/v7/vix-paper.csv \
    2022-12 2026-11 US XM NO SE HU NZ JP CA CH

Dukascopy H1 BID/ASK for the 8-pair traded basket plus already-seen
helpers, 2023-01 through the latest published month (2026-11 was HTTP 404
at the first run and was skipped by the v7.1 fetcher):
  python3 scripts/v7-fetch.py paper 2023-01 2026-11 \
    EURNOK EURSEK USDHUF EURHUF NZDJPY CADJPY CHFJPY EURNZD \
    USDJPY USDNOK USDSEK NZDUSD

Scale: 1e-3 for JPY and HUF quotes, 1e-5 otherwise.
Raw .bi5 and H1 CSVs are gitignored; rebuild with the command above.
Processed D1 (and H4) plus rates, VIX and the aligned market tables are
committed. sha256 is in data/v7/SHA256SUMS.
