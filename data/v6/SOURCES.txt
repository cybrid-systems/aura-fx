v6 training macro inputs (public, no login). Cut off strictly before 2024-01-01.
Raw Dukascopy month files and H1 CSVs are not in git (see .gitignore);
they are cached in data/raw-dukascopy/ and rebuilt by the commands below.

Policy rates (BIS WS_CBPOL, previous-month use, no look-ahead):
  python3 scripts/v6-rates.py data/v6/rates-train.csv data/v6/vix-train.csv \
    2014-12 2023-12 --cutoff 2024-01-01 US XM GB JP AU NZ CA CH

VIX (tried in order; this train pull used datasets/finance-vix CBOE close):
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/datasets/finance-vix/main/data/vix-daily.csv
  https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/datasets/finance-vix/data/vix-daily.csv
  https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=VIXCLS
  https://stooq.com/q/d/l/?s=^vix&i=d

Dukascopy H1 BID/ASK for the 7-pair training basket (2015-01..2023-12):
  python3 scripts/v6-fetch.py train 2015-01 2023-12 \
    EURUSD GBPUSD USDJPY AUDUSD NZDUSD USDCAD USDCHF

Final-test prices, EM policy rates (NO/SE/MX/ZA), and 2024+ VIX are
intentionally not downloaded until after the freeze.
